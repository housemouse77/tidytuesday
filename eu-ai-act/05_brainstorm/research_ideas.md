## Prompt questions to generate ideas (answer them, don't skip)
1. **Regulatory events as shocks.** The Act has staggered dates (Feb 2025, Aug 2025, Aug 2026, Omnibus delays). Which firms are differentially exposed, and what accounting outcome would move?
2. **Disclosure.** Do EU listed firms disclose AI risk or AI Act compliance in annual and sustainability reports? Did it change around these dates? What role does the CSRD/ESRS (double materiality) play?
3. **Assurance market.** Conformity assessment, ISO/IEC 42001 certification and AI audits. Who supplies them (Big 4, notified bodies, consultancies)? Is there pricing or concentration evidence?
4. **Financial institutions.** Annex III 5(b)/(c) makes credit scoring and insurance pricing high-risk. Do Italian banks change lending, provisioning (IFRS 9 ECL models) or model governance?
5. **Internal control.** How does the AI Act's QMS and risk management (Art. 9, 17) map onto COSO and internal-control reporting? Is there a "SOX moment" for AI?
6. **The intelligence lens.** National-security carve-outs, dual-use tools, and source-reliability methods applied to AI disclosure quality. What does your background let you see that accounting scholars cannot?
7. **Italy-specific.** L. 132/2025 goes beyond the Act in places. Is there cross-country variation in national implementation that could be exploited?
